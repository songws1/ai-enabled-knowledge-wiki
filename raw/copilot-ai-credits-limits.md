# Capture: AI credits and limits for Microsoft 365 subscriptions (Microsoft Support)

- Retrieval URL: https://support.microsoft.com/en-US/Microsoft-365-Copilot/ai-credits-and-limits-for-microsoft-365-subscriptions
- Capture date: 2026-09-07
- Capture method: WebFetch (verbatim-extraction prompt) against the live page. Excerpts are exact quotes as returned. Not a full-page archive.
- Publisher: Microsoft
- Note: this page covers paid Personal, Family, and Premium tiers. It does not state free-tier credit quotas. The absence is itself recorded below.

## Captured excerpts

What consumes AI credits:

> "For example, asking Copilot to 'summarize my inbox' would deduct one AI credit."

> "Your AI credits will deduct automatically as you use Copilot in Microsoft 365 and Windows apps" — listing Designer, Create, Photos, Paint, Notepad, Word, and Excel as examples.

Plan allotments (paid tiers only):

> 60 monthly image credits for Microsoft 365 Personal and Family; "Extensive usage beyond standard credit limits" for Microsoft 365 Premium.

Other daily limits listed (paid tiers): Vision 10 min/day Personal and Family, 15 min/day Premium; Voice 30 min/day, 60 min/day Premium; Audio Overview in Notebooks 6 uses/day Personal.

Exclusions:

> "Microsoft 365 Basic does not include advanced AI features."

> "AI benefits are only available to the subscription owner and cannot be shared with others."

What happens at the limit:

> "You will be notified when you reach your feature limits for AI features."

Variation over time and platform:

> "These limits may vary based on the feature, entry point, and system conditions."

> "Limits may also change over time as new AI models become available."

## Recorded absence

The fetch was asked specifically for credit limits for free users versus subscribers. No free-tier credit amounts appear on this page. Free-tier usage limits must be sourced from a page that names free users (see copilot-app-changes.md).
