## Captured excerpts

What free users can do:

> "You can continue to chat with Copilot, create images, upload files and more for free, … subject to available capacity and limits."

Free versus paid:

> "Microsoft 365 subscribers can get more done with Copilot, with advanced AI, leading models, … higher usage limits than free users."

> "Podcasts, Deep Research, Group chat, and other features are retiring."

> "Some features may be temporarily unavailable, or you may experience functionality gaps … while the latest version of Copilot is being rolled out."
