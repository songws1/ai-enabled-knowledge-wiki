## Who this is

Someone accountable for how a team performs a recurring operation. Generic role; no named individuals.

## What they are trying to do

Turn one person's working approach into the team's default: distributed, adopted, and checkable. Their success measure is not "it worked when I did it" but "it works the same way for everyone, and we can tell when it stops working".

## What they typically have access to

- The same free-tier tooling as their team (see [[copilot-free]]) — the constraint is the team's, not just theirs.
- Control over a shared team folder and the team's browser profiles or bookmarks.
- Authority to declare a standard, and responsibility for noticing when the standard drifts.

The material aimed at this role is distribution-focused: [[share-prompt-blocks-with-team]] is theirs; the path free-tier-starter is how they onboard someone into the method.
