## Who this is

Someone who runs recurring operational analysis — quality checks, summaries, small calculations — as part of another job. Generic role; no named individuals.

## What they are trying to do

Make the recurring work repeatable: same task, same method, same output shape every time, and hand the method to someone else when they are out. They do not want to do the task once; they want the task to be doable by the next person.

## What they typically have access to

- A free-tier AI chat tool in a browser (see [[copilot-free]] for what that includes and lacks).
- A shared team folder they can read and write.
- A spreadsheet application.
- No budget, no deployment rights, no admin privileges.

The material aimed at this role assumes all four of those at once: standardization without purchase ([[portable-prompt-template]]), and tools that arrive as files ([[generated-single-file-tool]]).
